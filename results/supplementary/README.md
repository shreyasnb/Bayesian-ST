## Canonical catalogue exports

Every remaining page of the sixteen individual source studies per application. This includes calibration, negative results, model mismatch, numerical accuracy, other conditions, constructed examples and optional overview compositions.

These are PDF copies or lossless PDF page extractions from the verified canonical catalogues. Pages retain seven-inch width, LaTeX/vector text and lines, and 300-dpi raster layers. Increasing dpi affects raster layers and previews; PDF vector text has no pixel resolution.

No simulation, inference, fitting, benchmark tuning or numerical data changes occur during this export. Main selections are editorial choices made while preparing the manuscript; evaluated episodes and methods are unchanged. Every individual source page appears in exactly one of these two folders.

The three original supplementary bundles are not copied because they repeat individual pages, including main candidates. Complete original catalogues remain in `results/beamforming/` and `results/contact/` for the notebooks. Exact selectors, source hashes, captions and output hashes are in [the export manifest](../draft_figure_manifest.json).
