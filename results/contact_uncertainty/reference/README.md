# Full-density process reference

This reference filters the probability density on unoriented normal lines, without replacing its posterior by a Bingham law. Even spherical harmonics represent the previous density. The rotationally invariant Watson transition acts diagonally on their coefficients by the Funk–Hecke identity; correction multiplies by the original Gaussian likelihood. There is no dense matrix of pairwise spherical transition probabilities.

The development experiment uses four independent trajectories for each of kappa = 4 and 20, with reports at steps 8, 16 and 24. Its random root, analysis budgets and acceptance thresholds are recorded in `development_config.json`. Final matched evaluation uses a separate root. Harmonic degree 24 is checked against degree 32, and scalar/region summaries use polar orders 128 and 192, with twice as many azimuth angles. These comparisons are empirical refinement checks, not rigorous error bounds.

`checks.json` and `development_checks.csv` refer to the final source hashes. All 24 development targets pass. The maximum mean difference is 1.68e-14, total-variation difference 1.51e-8, region-mass difference 0.000639, scalar-CDF difference 0.00276 and interval-endpoint difference relative to interval width 0.0129.

The earlier development attempts are retained in subdirectories. In particular, `development_axis_grid/` records an unresolved scalar-CDF comparison. A physical azimuth grid puts an artificial ring of quadrature mass at a zero normal component, which is inaccurate near the endpoint of the diagonal response 1 - n_i^2. The corrected implementation rotates the integration polar axis to i using an exact coordinate transformation within each harmonic degree. It integrates the regular coordinate |n_i| and transforms its quantiles back to response coordinates. Off-diagonal response CDFs use coalesced, monotone midpoint-mass interpolation on their physical support. Both calculations retain independent-grid refinement checks.

The final matched study predeclares the first sixteen trajectory identifiers at each kappa for this full recursive reference. Other matched trajectories compare the moment-matched Bingham approximation, its same-parameter Laplace approximation, and the recursive tangent-Gaussian filter. Any final reference refinement failure remains in its output tables and limits conclusions about distribution shape and tails.
